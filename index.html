How the calculation works

Available filament = total spool weight − empty spool weight.

Surplus / shortfall = available filament − print requirement.

Reserve headroom = available filament − print requirement − reserve. Zero or more means the print fits while preserving your reserve.

Use non-negative weights with up to three decimal places (a decimal point), up to 1,000,000 g. Equal weights are accepted; a zero print requirement is valid.
